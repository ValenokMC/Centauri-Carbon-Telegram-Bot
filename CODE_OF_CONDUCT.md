# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about 3D printing; there is nothing here
worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help, or trying to be helped.
- Criticise the code, the documentation or the idea. Not the person.
- Remember that most people here are not professional programmers, and that
  "obvious" is a word describing you, not the thing.
- Accept that the maintainer is one person with a day job, and that "no" is a
  legitimate answer to a feature request.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, discussions, pull requests, or Telegram.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including posting a screenshot with
  somebody's token, chat id, or home IP address visible.
- Deliberately wasting people's time: spam, bad-faith argument, or reopening a
  settled decision over and over.

## Scope

This applies across the repository — issues, discussions, pull requests, commit
comments — and in the project's Telegram support channel.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to
[@SupporBiBot](https://t.me/SupporBiBot?start=centauri_bot). Do not use a public
issue for a report that names someone.

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close
threads, or block accounts, and is not obliged to explain a block publicly.
